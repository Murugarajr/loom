Design Notes

Skeuomorphic Platinum Deck

Brushed metallic console chassis with chamfered specular highlights, precision bevels, and tactile push buttons inspired by PowerDVD.

Backlit Cyan VFD Telemetry

Deep recessed high-contrast screen housing glowing cyan digital numerals (00:24 / 01:51) with authentic phosphor glow and high contrast over any scene.

Optical Cyber-Thread & Jog Wheel

Electric cyan laser seek rail with chrome capsule shuttle bead, matched with a concentric machined aluminum jog dial.

Contra Challenge Compliance & Verification

Works: Powered by official Video.js 10 (@videojs/html 10.4.0) with standard web components, playing the NASA Cosmic Dawn HLS stream with chapters and subtitles.

Essential Controls: Play/pause (jog dial & bottom bar), seek slider, digital timecode, vertical volume popover/mute, subtitles, settings menu, and fullscreen.

Accessibility & Keyboard: Unmissable #00f0ff electric cyan focus outline (2px with 10px glow), 44×44px minimum touch targets on mobile pointers, and instant animations when prefers-reduced-motion: reduce is active.

Responsiveness: Fluidly responsive across widths from 240px and 340px up to ultra-wide displays without horizontal clipping or overflow.

Demo video: NASA Cosmic Dawn Trailer, served via Mux HLS. Built with Video.js 10 for the Contra Video.js Skins Design Challenge.
