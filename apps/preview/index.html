Design Notes

Illuminated thread

A radiant vermilion fiber-optic core with knurled diagonal weave, ambient drop glow, and a quiet buffered strand.

Machined jewel shuttle

A milled brass/titanium coin with an illuminated center jewel dot, providing tactile haptic and sub-pixel hover response.

Floating titanium chassis

An elevated island with 24px backdrop blur, sub-pixel specular highlight, concentric milled puck controls, and focus-aware keyboard lock.

Verification and known limitations

Verified in Chrome during development. Keyboard focus retention, responsive caption clearance across widths from 240 to 960 px, and menu containment have been verified in automated Chrome test suites.

Known fallback limitation: Centre-group suppression relies on CSS :has(). In environments lacking :has() support, centre controls remain visible and may collide with lifted captions at narrow widths (240 px and 340 px).

Fullscreen (including captions), physical touch, RTL languages, real Windows High Contrast, and screen-reader output remain explicitly unverified.
